About us

Who are we?

We are Apex Racing, a STEM Racing team from Matthew Arnold School, Oxford, formed in 2024 we have participated in both entry and development class of STEM Racing, acheiveing many awards and reaching the uk national finals last year, this year we hope to continue that with our development into the professional class of the compeition.









About the car

This is MAS 25, our professional class car, manufactured from a low density polyurethane block the car weighs only 48g and travels up to 40mph, powered by only a CO2 Canister. This car is the result of months of development, including over 30 full car CFD tests preformed in state of the art CFD software and real life testing at Aston Universitys test track. In addition to this the car is made up of extremely strong, lightweight resin courtesy of one of our sponsors, 3Dresyns (read more about our sponsors here)
