Our gold tier sponsors

None of what we do could be possible without
the immense help from our sponsors.



E3D are a sponsor who has been with the team for 3 years, they are our primary monetary sponsor without them we would not be here. E3D are a company based in Oxfordshire like us. They are the world's leading 3D printer hotend developer and manufacturer.






3Dresyns are our Additive materials partner. They provide us with their innovative performance resins for SLA printing. The resin they supply is odorless and has no fumes, for every bottle a tree is grown. The strength and properties is unparalleled and can be in some cases twice as strong as FDM filament. We highly recommend their products.







Ansys is a global leader in simulations. They gave us a set of licenses for their simulation suite for free, we opted for the full ansys version over the student version to enable us to mesh at a higher quality and get a higher accuracy and better analysis. Our development process would not be possible without ansys and their support.
